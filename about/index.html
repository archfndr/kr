ABOUT ARCHFNDR

Human taste, kept visible.

ArchFndr is a personal curation project for music worth returning to — not an endless feed, and not a recommendation engine.

The visual index is for discovery. The archive is for memory. Each track opens into a short note and the places where it can be heard.
